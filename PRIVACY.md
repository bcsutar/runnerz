# Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 2, 2026

Runnerz: FTMS Companion ("Runnerz") is an Apple Watch app for compatible Bluetooth FTMS treadmills.

## Information Runnerz Uses

Runnerz accesses health and fitness data through Apple HealthKit, including heart rate and workout metrics. Runnerz also receives treadmill data over Bluetooth, such as speed, distance, and incline when the treadmill reports it.

This information is used only to display your workout, calculate workout metrics, and save completed workouts to Apple Health after you confirm the save action.

## Information Runnerz Collects

Runnerz does not operate a server and does not transmit your health, fitness, Bluetooth, or workout data to the developer. Runnerz does not use analytics, advertising, tracking, accounts, or cloud storage. Runnerz does not sell or share your data.

Workout data saved by Runnerz is stored in Apple Health and is subject to Apple's Health privacy practices. You control Runnerz's access through the Health and Privacy settings on your paired iPhone.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when Runnerz's data practices change. The latest version will always be available at this URL.
